What Are Fake Loan Apps, and Why Are They So Dangerous?

Fake loan apps offer quick money with zero paperwork — but they’re traps. They often steal your data, charge hidden high interest, and harass you for repayment within days.

Why Are They Dangerous?

  • Access your contacts, photos, and messages
  • Use threats and blackmail
  • No RBI approval
  • Create a cycle of debt using multiple apps

What Should You Do?

  • Don’t panic or pay under pressure
  • Report on cybercrime.gov.in
  • Avoid sharing personal data
  • Contact CutMyLoan for legal support

How CutMyLoan Helps

We help you fight harassment legally, report fake apps, and protect your financial dignity.

Privacy Policy – CutMyLoan

CutMyLoan.com (“CutMyLoan”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal and financial information you share with us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, share, and safeguard your data, along with your rights and choices.

By accessing or using our website, forms, or services, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect two types of information:

a. Personally Identifiable Information (PII)

Collected when you:

  • Fill a form on our website (via HubSpot)
  • Communicate with our advisors (via call Email or WhatsApp)
  • Subscribe to our services

This includes:

  • Full Name
  • Phone Number
  • Email Address
  • Financial Data (e.g., existing loan details, credit card debt, EMI history, liabilities)

b. Non-Personal Data

Automatically collected through tools like Google Analytics:

  • Browser and Device Type
  • Time Zone and IP Address
  • Pages Visited
  • Language Preference
  • Referring URL

2. How We Collect Data

  • HubSpot Form: For capturing basic contact details (name, phone, email).
  • Call/WhatsApp Follow-ups: Advisors collect financial information directly from you during conversations.
  • Google Analytics: Helps us understand website usage and improve user experience.

3. Use of Your Information

We use your data to:

  • Provide consultation on debt settlement
  • Customize solutions based on your financial profile
  • Communicate via email, phone, or WhatsApp
  • Send relevant service updates and offers
  • Improve our website, services, and lead quality
  • Perform internal analysis and reporting

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or rent your information to any third party.

We may only share data with:

  • Credit Bureaus (e.g., CIBIL, Experian) — with your consent, to assess your financial standing
  • Internal Advisors — bound by confidentiality agreements
  • Legal Authorities — if required by law, legal process, or for fraud prevention

5. Data Security

Your data security is our top priority:

  • We use SSL encryption for all transmissions
  • Client data is stored securely on internal company servers
  • Only limited and authorized team members have access
  • Our website is regularly scanned for vulnerabilities
  • Payment data, if any, is processed through secure third-party gateways (we do not store card data)

6. Data Retention and Deletion Policy

We retain your information only during the course of service.

All personal and financial data is automatically deleted within 90 days (3 months) after your service with CutMyLoan ends.

This ensures your long-term privacy and data protection.

7. Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • Request access to your information
  • Ask for corrections or deletion

8. Use of Cookies

We may use cookies and tracking pixels for:

  • Website analytics
  • Customization
  • Retargeted advertising

You can choose to disable cookies from your browser settings.

9. Third-Party Links

Our website may include links to external sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. Please review their respective policies when visiting.

10. Updates to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Continued use of our website means you accept the latest version.
